- Best Python BLE library: `govee-ble` (PyPI, Bluetooth-Devices org, 850+ dependents)
- H6006 uses standard Govee BLE protocol (same as H6001/H6127)
- BLE Service UUID: 00010203-0405-0607-0809-0a0b0c0d1910
- Write Characteristic: 00010203-0405-0607-0809-0a0b0c0d2b11
- 20-byte packets: [0x33][command][payload...][XOR checksum]
- Commands: power (0x01), brightness (0x04), color/mode (0x05)
